Updated 12.5.2026

ReittiGPS does not collect or store personal information such as name, email address, age, gender or date of birth.

ReittiGPS uses a third-party service (Sentry) to collect privacy-minimized crash reports, error reports, and technical diagnostics in order to identify and fix errors and monitor app reliability. This may include technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, crash stack trace, limited app diagnostic state at the time of an error, and pseudonymous session health information such as whether an app session ended normally or crashed. ReittiGPS does not send Sentry your name, email address, saved addresses, route searches, precise GPS location, or advertising identifiers. It may send a coarse regional status, such as whether the app is operating in Helsinki-region or nationwide mode, when relevant for diagnosing app behavior. See Sentry’s privacy policy for further details: https://sentry.io/privacy/

ReittiGPS uses the API provided by the Helsinki Region Transport authority to offer journey planning services. Their privacy policy can be found at https://www.hsl.fi/en/privacy although it is mostly irrelevant for ReittiGPS because the app doesn't collect nor provide personal information such as name, email address, age, gender or date of birth. HSL does receive the IP address of your device, as well as any location or address information you may provide when asking the app to plan your journey.

ReittiGPS itself doesn't have any server-side components and does not use third-party tracking libraries for analytics or advertising purposes.

Updated 12.5.2026

ReittiGPS does not collect or store personal information such as name, email address, age, gender or date of birth.

ReittiGPS uses a third-party service (Sentry) to collect privacy-minimized crash reports, error reports, and technical diagnostics in order to identify and fix errors and monitor app reliability. This may include technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, crash stack trace, limited app diagnostic state at the time of an error, pseudonymous session health information such as whether an app session ended normally or crashed, and a random app installation identifier used to understand how many installations are affected by an error. ReittiGPS does not send Sentry your name, email address, saved addresses, route searches, precise GPS coordinates, advertising identifiers, or Apple device identifiers. ReittiGPS configures Sentry not to collect default personal information and not to store IP addresses in event data. Like other internet services, Sentry may process connection information such as IP address as necessary to receive and operate the service. See Sentry’s privacy policy for further details: https://sentry.io/privacy/

ReittiGPS uses the API provided by the Helsinki Region Transport authority to offer journey planning services. Their privacy policy can be found at https://www.hsl.fi/en/privacy although it is mostly irrelevant for ReittiGPS because the app doesn't collect nor provide personal information such as name, email address, age, gender or date of birth. HSL does receive the IP address of your device, as well as any location or address information you may provide when asking the app to plan your journey.

ReittiGPS itself doesn't have any server-side components and does not use third-party tracking libraries for analytics or advertising purposes.
